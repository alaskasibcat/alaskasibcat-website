About us

Our cattery

We are ALASKASIBCAT, a small home cattery of Siberian cats in Wasilla, Alaska.

We have lived with Siberians for many years, and they still surprise us with their intelligence, quiet dignity and individual ways of being close to people.

Our home is in Alaska's Matanuska-Susitna Valley, among mountains and forests. We love the way Siberians belong in that landscape: strong, beautiful northern cats who are also very much part of home life.

Each cat is a personality

Some greet you at the door and follow you from room to room. Others prefer a quiet place nearby, watching everything that happens. A kitten's temperament matters to us just as much as its appearance.

Our breeding cats came from Eastern Europe. We choose our pairs thoughtfully, pay close attention to our cats' health and work to preserve the traditional, natural type of the breed. ALASKASIBCAT is registered with CFA (cattery No. 307679) and TICA (cattery No. 101972).

Raised at home

Our cattery is small by choice. From their first days, kittens live in our home and become familiar with people, voices, gentle handling and the ordinary sounds of a household.

Getting to know them as they grow is one of the most rewarding parts of this work. We want to help each kitten find a family whose life and temperament are a good match.

Finding your Siberian

Tell us who lives in your home, whether you have children or other animals, and what your days are like. There is no single personality that fits every family, so we are glad to talk through what you are looking for.

If allergies are a concern, please tell us before deciding. Every person and every cat is different; we do not guarantee allergy compatibility.
