About us

Hello, and let us introduce ourselves.

We live in the Matanuska-Susitna Valley of Alaska, among mountains, forests and the kind of quiet you only find in the North. This is where our Siberians live, far from any bustle. And honestly, the place is a lot like the cats themselves: strong, beautiful, and very warm once you get to know it.

The Siberian is a breed in which strength and grace share one body. A powerful frame, a luxurious triple coat, an intelligent and attentive gaze, and a character that melts your heart. These cats do not fuss over small things. They are calm and unflappable, with an innate sense of tact and a quiet dignity of their own. They watch you closely and join family life on their own terms. Today on your lap, tomorrow on the top shelf, like a queen on her throne. Siberians are deeply loyal, true family cats, but they show it each in their own way. One will meet you at the door and follow you from room to room, chatting in purrs; another prefers to keep a little distance and simply be nearby, in the same room, watching. Each one has a temperament of its own, and that is the charm of it. I have lived with Siberians for many years and they still amaze me. However many I raise, every one finds a way to surprise me: with intelligence, with tact, with some particular way of looking at the world. These are not simply beautiful cats. They are personalities.

It all began long before us. The Siberian was shaped by nature, not drawn up in a breeder’s office. For centuries these cats took form in the harsh climate of Siberia: the strongest survived, immunity hardened, and the genetics grew sound. The result is a cat with a sturdy frame, excellent health and real endurance, a cat nobody tries to “improve”, because there is nothing to improve. Our task is to preserve, with care, what Siberia created: we choose our pairs thoughtfully, keep a close eye on the health of our breeding cats, and hold to the traditional, natural type of the breed.

That is also why all of our breeding cats were imported from Eastern Europe, the homeland of the breed, from long-established catteries with proven, reliable lines. The cattery is registered with TICA and CFA, and for us that is not a line in a document but a daily responsibility to every cat and to every family that takes one of our kittens home.

We have one more special interest. The protein that most often triggers cat allergies is called Fel d 1, and some Siberians naturally produce less of it than others. We are deliberately working with lower-Fel d 1 lines so that, one day, people who thought a cat was out of reach may be able to hug a Siberian of their own. We will not promise what no one can promise: every cat is different, and so is every person. But if allergies are a concern, tell us. We will be honest with you, and we can help you find out whether a particular kitten might be a good match.

Cats are not a hobby for me; they are my life. That is why our cattery is small, by choice. From the very first day a kitten lives in our home, among people: it gets used to hands, voices and the ordinary noise of a household, the way a caring family raises a newborn. Our kittens are not “a product from a cattery”. They are home-raised children, ready to become yours: the power and natural stature of a northern cat, multiplied by deep home socialization.

We would love to help you find your own: the loyal, strong and noble friend who will fit into your life and match you in character and temperament. Tell us a little about yourselves: who lives in your home, whether there are children or other animals, what your days look like. The more we know, the better the match, and that is the most enjoyable part of our work.

How to adopt a kitten

  1. Get in touch. Tell us a little about your home and what you are looking for.
  2. Choose a kitten. We will share current information and answer questions.
  3. Reserve and plan the handover. A $350 reservation deposit, credited toward the price, reserves the kitten; pickup or delivery is arranged with you directly.
