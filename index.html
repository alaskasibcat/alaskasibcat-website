Our cattery

A small home cattery in Alaska

We are ALASKASIBCAT, a small home cattery in Wasilla, in Alaska's Matanuska-Susitna Valley. Our Siberians grow up at home, among people, familiar voices and the everyday sounds of family life.

What we love most is how different they are. One follows you from room to room; another is happiest sitting quietly nearby. Getting to know each kitten's personality helps us find a home that suits them.

Tell us a little about your household and the companion you are looking for. We would love to help you find your Siberian.

How to adopt a kitten

  1. Get in touch. Tell us a little about your home and what you are looking for.
  2. Choose a kitten. We will share current information and answer questions.
  3. Reserve and plan the handover. A $350 reservation deposit, credited toward the price, reserves the kitten; pickup or delivery is arranged with you directly.
