How to adopt a kitten

  1. Get in touch. Tell us a little about your home and what you are looking for.
  2. Choose a kitten. We will share current information and answer questions.
  3. Reserve and plan the handover. A $350 deposit reserves the kitten; pickup or delivery is arranged with you directly.

This page is a plain-language summary. The signed purchase agreement is the binding document, and its terms take precedence over anything written here.

Price and reservation

Pet kittens are $1,600–$2,900. The price of each kitten is listed with the kitten, and we are happy to answer any question about it.

A deposit of $350 reserves a kitten. The deposit is non-refundable if the buyer cancels. If the cattery cannot complete the sale, the deposit is returned in full. The balance is paid before the kitten goes home.

When kittens go home

Kittens leave for their new homes at about 4 months of age, after they have been spayed or neutered. Our kittens are placed as pets, not for breeding.

What is included

  • Age-appropriate vaccinations
  • Microchip
  • Spay or neuter surgery
  • Parasite treatment
  • Veterinary health check
  • A starter supply of the food the kitten is used to, a toy, and a small gift from us

Documents

  • Signed purchase agreement
  • CFA registration or pedigree
  • Veterinary health record

Our 2-year health guarantee

Every kitten goes home with a written 2-year health guarantee. We are confident in the health of our lines, and we put it in writing.

  • Each kitten is healthy at the time of transfer, vaccinated, microchipped, and free of FeLV, FIV and common parasites.
  • For 2 years from the day the kitten goes home, it is covered against life-threatening hereditary and congenital defects. If a kitten dies as the direct result of such a defect, we provide a replacement pet kitten of comparable quality within 12 months.
  • Your part is simple: have the kitten examined by a licensed veterinarian within 3 calendar days after pickup or delivery, and let us know in writing if anything is found. This first visit activates the guarantee.

Full conditions, required proof and exclusions are set out in the purchase agreement, which you receive and sign before the kitten goes home.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is in Wasilla, Alaska. We also arrange delivery to other states; delivery options are discussed individually, and the cost of transportation is paid by the buyer and is not included in the price.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit the kittens?
Please contact us to arrange it. We raise our kittens at home, so visits are planned in advance.
Are Siberian cats hypoallergenic?
No cat is completely allergen-free. Many people with mild cat allergies report fewer symptoms with Siberians, but reactions vary from person to person. If allergies are a concern, we recommend spending time with the breed before deciding.
Can I reserve a kitten from a future litter?
Yes. Write to us and we will let you know when a litter is confirmed.
Do you sell kittens for breeding?
No. Our kittens are placed as pets and are spayed or neutered before they go home.
Can the kitten go outside?
Our kittens are indoor cats. Outdoor time is fine under supervision, on a leash or in a secure enclosure.
What if I can no longer keep the cat?
Please contact us first. We ask every owner to offer the cat back to us before rehoming it, so it never ends up in a shelter.
